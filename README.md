# Hi, I'm Francesco 👋

**AI Engineer at Datapizza · Mathematics and Computer Science background**

I design and build GenAI systems, with experience across conversational AI,
document retrieval, data engineering and mathematical optimization.

A recurring question in my work: **how do we make useful AI systems behave within
clear boundaries?** I care about explicit responsibilities, evaluation, and the
engineering around the model.

[Portfolio](https://meguazy.github.io/en/) · [Projects](https://meguazy.github.io/en/projects/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/francesco-finucci-b096bb219/)

## What I work on

- **GenAI and retrieval:** document processing, vector search, reranking, APIs and evaluation.
- **Conversational systems:** connecting AI workflows with retrieval, application APIs and voice/avatar interfaces.
- **Development with agents:** keeping requirements, architecture decisions, implementation and evidence aligned.
- **Optimization:** translating operating constraints into mathematical models and working software.

Before Datapizza, I worked as a contract researcher at the **University of Camerino**,
contributing to conversational AI and document retrieval systems, and designing and
developing a mixed-integer scheduling optimizer. My earlier work spans data engineering
and backend development.
